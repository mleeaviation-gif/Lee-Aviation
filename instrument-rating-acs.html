Instrument Rating – Airplane ACS Study Guide

A task-by-task reading guide for the current Instrument Rating – Airplane Airman Certification Standards (FAA-S-ACS-8C). It follows the same resource-link format as the Private/Commercial guide and points students toward current regulations, the AIM, FAA handbooks, current charts/publications, and selected advisory circulars.

IRApplies to the instrument-airplane practical testMEMultiengine-only taskReferences: eCFR · AIM · IFH · IPH · PHAK · AFH · AWH · AC

The official ACS controls. This page is a study cross-reference, not an FAA-issued item-by-item crosswalk. The ACS reference list sometimes names an advisory circular without its later revision letter; where practical, the links below point to the current revision. Always verify current regulations, AIM changes, NOTAMs, chart cycles, navigation databases, and the aircraft POH/AFM.

Aircraft-specific references: Several ACS tasks explicitly depend on the airplane’s POH/AFM, avionics supplements, limitations, and checklists. Those cannot be linked generically, so “Aircraft POH/AFM” buttons below are reminders to add the exact documents for the airplane used in training/checkride preparation.
