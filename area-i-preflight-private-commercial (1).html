Area of Operation I: Preflight Preparation

A shared Private and Commercial Airplane ACS reading guide. FAA handbook PDF links open at the printed page where the selected section begins; AIM links target the relevant numbered paragraph.

PPrivate pilotCCommercial pilotP+CApplies to bothReferences: PHAK · AFH · AWH · AIM · AC · eCFR

P / C colors identify ACS applicability, not whether every aircraft-specific item applies to every airplane. PHAK and AFH page links use each chapter’s PDF numbering (for example, PHAK 7-25 opens PDF page 25). The 2026 Aviation Weather Handbook is one PDF: its links name the relevant section and request a PDF text search; some viewers may ignore that request, so use the section name/bookmarks if needed. AIM links use official FAA HTML text highlighting when supported; otherwise the correct AIM section opens. Selected references are study aids, not an official item-by-item FAA crosswalk. Verify current regulations, handbook addenda, the aircraft POH/AFM and applicable limitations.
