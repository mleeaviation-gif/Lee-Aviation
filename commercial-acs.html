Risk Management:
  • Low-altitude maneuvering, stall/spin risks, and windshear on final[cite: 1]
  • Delayed go-around decisions and runway length constraints[cite: 1]
Skills:
  • Execute smooth takeoffs and stabilized approaches to precise touchdown points[cite: 1]
  • Maintain directional control and proper drift correction throughout[cite: 1]

Steep Turns, Chandelles, Lazy Eights, Eights on Pylons

  • Uncoordinated flight, accelerated stalls, and low-altitude terrain hazards[cite: 1]
Skills:

  • Execute graceful commercial maneuvers demonstrating precise energy management[cite: 1]

Slow Flight, Stalls, Spin Awareness
