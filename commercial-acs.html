Commercial Pilot ACS Master Knowledge, Risk & Skill Index

Comprehensive mapping of all Areas of Operation, encompassing required Knowledge (K), Risk Management (R), and Skill (S) elements[cite: 1].

Area of Operation Tasks & Core Elements (K / R / S) FAA Reference Links
Area of Operation I: Preflight Preparation
Task A-C Knowledge: Privileges, limitations, currency, airworthiness, weather products/theory.
Risk: Currency vs. proficiency, go/no-go decisions, weather minimums.
Skills: PIC VFR application, airworthiness verification, weather briefing analysis.		 14 CFR Part 61 14 CFR Part 91 PHAK Ch 12 (Weather)
Task D-I Knowledge: Cross-country planning, NAS airspace, performance data, aircraft systems, human factors.
Risk: External pressures, airspace violations, performance discrepancies, fatigue/hypoxia.
Skills: Flight plan generation, weight & balance computation, system operation.		 PHAK Ch 11 (Performance) PHAK Ch 17 (Aeromedical)
Area of Operation II: Preflight Procedures
Task A-F Knowledge: Pilot self-assessment, preflight inspections, passenger briefings, engine starting, taxi/sailing rules, before-takeoff checks.
Risk: Runway incursions, propeller safety, checklist distraction, expectation bias.
Skills: Thorough aircraft inspection, flight deck management, positive aircraft ground control.		 AFH Ch 2 (Ground Ops)
Area of Operation III: Airport and Seaplane Base Operations
Task A-B Knowledge: Radio communications, ATC light signals, runway lighting, traffic pattern operations, right-of-way rules.
Risk: Collision hazards, windshear, wake turbulence, communication loss.
Skills: Standard phraseology, traffic pattern adherence ($\pm100$ ft, $\pm10$ kts).		 AFH Ch 7 (Airport Ops)
Area of Operation IV: Takeoffs, Landings, and Go-Arounds
Task A-F Knowledge: Stabilized approaches, energy management, crosswind components, best angle/rate of speeds ($V_X / V_Y$), soft/short field dynamics.
Risk: Low-altitude stalls, crosswind drift, runway length limits, delayed go-arounds.
Skills: Precise takeoff control, targeted touchdown execution (within 100-200 ft).		 AFH Ch 8 (Takeoffs) AFH Ch 9 (Landings)
Task G-N Knowledge: Water/confined area operations, glassy/rough water techniques, power-off $180^\circ$ accuracy approach objectives.
Risk: Porpoising/skipping, unseen water hazards, slip mechanics, tail stalls with flaps.
Skills: Accurate power-off gliding approach to a designated landing spot.		 AFH Ch 9 (Approaches & Landings)
Area of Operation V: Performance Maneuvers and Ground Reference Maneuvers
Task A-E Knowledge: Steep turn aerodynamics, overbanking tendencies, load factor, pivotal altitude for eights on pylons, chandelles, lazy eights.
Risk: Uncoordinated flight, altitude loss, accelerated stalls, division of attention.
Skills: Smooth execution maintaining bank ($\pm5^\circ$) and altitude ($\pm100$ ft).		 AFH Ch 10 (Commercial Maneuvers)
Area of Operation VI: Navigation
Task A-D Knowledge: Pilotage, dead reckoning, compass errors, ground/satellite navigation systems, diversion planning, lost procedures.
Risk: Spatial disorientation, fuel exhaustion, loss of signal, ATC radar limitations.
Skills: Course tracking, checkpoint timing ($\pm3$ mins), prompt diversion.		 PHAK Ch 16 (Navigation)
Area of Operation VII: Slow Flight and Stalls
Task A-E Knowledge: Flight at minimum controllable airspeed, power-on/off stalls, accelerated stall dynamics, spin awareness and recovery.
Risk: Inadvertent stall/spin, secondary stalls, cross-control slips close to the ground.
Skills: Prompt recognition of first indication of stall and immediate clean recovery.		 AFH Ch 5 (Slow Flight & Stalls)
Area of Operation VIII: High-Altitude Operations
Task A-B Knowledge: Regulatory supplemental oxygen requirements, hypoxia symptoms, time of useful consciousness (TUC), pressurization systems.
Risk: High-altitude fatigue, rapid decompression hazards, oxygen cylinder fire risks.
Skills: Oxygen quantity calculation, passenger briefing simulation.		 PHAK Ch 17 (Aeromedical Factors)
Area of Operation IX: Emergency Operations
Task A-G Knowledge: Emergency descent triggers, best glide speeds vs. minimum sink, system/instrument malfunctions, multiengine engine-out procedures ($V_{MC}$, $V_{YSE}$).
Risk: Startle response, checklist rushing, low-altitude engine failure management.
Skills: Positive aircraft control, checklist execution, simulated engine-out handling.		 AFH Ch 18 (Emergency Procedures) AFH Ch 13 (Multiengine Ops)
Area of Operation X: Multiengine Operations
Task A-D Knowledge: Factors affecting $V_{MC}$, zero sideslip concept, single-engine instrument flight and approaches.
Risk: Loss of directional control below $V_{MC}$, inability to maintain altitude on one engine.
Skills: Single-engine control tracking ($\pm100$ ft, $\pm10$ kts), $V_{MC}$ demonstration recovery.		 AFH Ch 13 (Multiengine Operations)
Area of Operation XI: Postflight Procedures
Task A-B Knowledge: Aircraft shutdown protocols, documenting maintenance discrepancies, seaplane mooring/anchoring.
Risk: Ramp distractions, safe passenger disembarkation around active props.
Skills: Proper parking, postflight inspection, securing the aircraft.		 AFH Ch 2 (Ground Ops)
