Why I Fly

Flying is unique in my view. A highly visceral experinence, especially tailwheel, where every aspect of a person comes into play. Pilots get to use their mind as well as their body to turn a science into an art. All while having a darn good time.

Flight Training Services

Private Pilot Certificate

Comprehensive primary instruction to earn your wings and safely navigate the skies.

  • FAA Minimum: 40 flight hours
  • National Average: ~70 flight hours

Commercial Pilot Certificate

Elevate your flying to the professional level with advanced maneuvers, precision airmanship, and mastery of commercial operations.

  • FAA Minimum: 250 total flight hours
  • Prerequisite: Private Pilot Certificate

Tailwheel Endorsement

Master conventional gear aircraft, energy management, and advanced stick-and-rudder techniques.

  • FAA Minimum: Proficiency-based
  • Average Time: 5–10 flight hours

Flight Review (BFR)

In lieu of a flight review I recommend the WINGS program. This resets your currency on a rolling basis and helps keep your proficinet year—round. The ground can be done on your own as well!

  • FAA Minimum: 1 hr ground / 1 hr flight
  • Average Time: 1.5 hrs ground / 1.5 hrs flight

FAA WINGS Program

Stay proficient and enhance your safety through the FAA's ongoing pilot proficiency program.

  • Requirement: 3 Knowledge & 3 Flight Activities
  • Benefit: Satisfies Flight Review (BFR)

Private Pilot Syllabus

Our training follows the structured Gleim Private Pilot Syllabus, ensuring a safe, building-block progression of learning from your first flight to your checkride.

Lesson Topic
STAGE ONE: Pre-Solo
Ground 1 Airplanes and Aerodynamics
Flight 1 Introduction to Flight
Flight 2 Four Fundamentals of Flight
Ground 2 Airplane Instruments, Engines, and Systems
Flight 4 Slow Flight and Stalls
Flight 11 First Solo
Flight 12 Stage One Check
STAGE TWO: Cross-Country & Preparation
Ground 7 Aviation Weather
Flight 14 Short-Field and Soft-Field Takeoffs and Landings
Flight 17 Dual Cross-Country
Ground 11 Cross-Country Flight Planning
Flight 25 Stage Two Check
Flight 26 End of Course Test
